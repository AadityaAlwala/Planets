Facts About Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only planet currently known to support life. Earth has large amounts of liquid water on its surface, with oceans covering about 71 percent of the planet. Earth also has a protective atmosphere that contains the oxygen that many living things need.

Earth is located in the habitable zone, sometimes called the Goldilocks zone. This is the region around a star where conditions can be suitable for liquid water to exist on a planet's surface. Earth is the right distance from the Sun to have temperatures that allow liquid water to remain on its surface.

Earth takes about 365 days to orbit the Sun and about 24 hours to rotate once on its axis. Earth has one natural satellite, the Moon. The Moon orbits Earth and affects ocean tides. Earth is also the largest of the four rocky planets in our solar system.
