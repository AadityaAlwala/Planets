Facts About Jupiter

Jupiter is the fifth planet from the Sun and is the largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter is so large that more than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it by volume.

Jupiter is famous for its Great Red Spot, which is a huge storm that has been observed for hundreds of years. The storm is larger than Earth and has very powerful winds. Jupiter also has colorful bands of clouds that circle the planet.

Jupiter has many moons, including four large moons called Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto. These four moons are known as the Galilean moons. Ganymede is the largest moon in our solar system and is even larger than the planet Mercury.
