Facts About Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because of its reddish color. The red color comes from iron minerals in the soil and rocks on its surface. Mars is a rocky planet and is about half the size of Earth.

Mars has a thin atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide. The planet is much colder than Earth, and its surface has mountains, valleys, volcanoes, and many craters. Mars also has the largest volcano in the solar system, Olympus Mons.

Mars has two small moons named Phobos and Deimos. A day on Mars is very similar to a day on Earth, lasting about 24 hours and 37 minutes. Scientists have sent robotic rovers and spacecraft to Mars to study its rocks, soil, atmosphere, and the possibility that the planet once had conditions suitable for life.
