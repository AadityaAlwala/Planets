Facts About Mercury

Mercury is the first planet from the Sun and the smallest planet in our solar system. It is only a little larger than Earth's Moon. Mercury is also the closest planet to the Sun, with an average distance of about 36 million miles from the Sun.

Mercury has a very unusual temperature difference between its day and night sides. During the day, temperatures can become extremely hot, while nighttime temperatures can become extremely cold. Mercury has almost no atmosphere to hold heat in, which causes these large temperature changes.

Mercury is the fastest planet to orbit the Sun. It travels around the Sun at an average speed of about 29 miles per second and completes one orbit in only 88 Earth days. Mercury also has many craters on its surface because it has been hit by asteroids and other space objects over billions of years.
