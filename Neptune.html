Facts About Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and farthest planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant with a deep blue color caused partly by methane in its atmosphere. Neptune is very cold and has some of the fastest winds in our solar system.

Neptune has huge storms and powerful winds that can reach more than 1,000 miles per hour. Scientists have observed large dark storms moving through Neptune's atmosphere. The planet takes about 165 Earth years to complete one trip around the Sun.

Neptune has many moons, with Triton being its largest moon. Triton is unusual because it orbits Neptune in the opposite direction from Neptune's rotation. Triton is also extremely cold and has a surface made mostly of frozen nitrogen and ice.
