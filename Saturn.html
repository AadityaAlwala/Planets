Facts About Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is the second-largest planet in our solar system. Saturn is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings, which are made mostly of pieces of ice, rock, and dust.

Saturn has a huge ring system made up of thousands of individual rings. Some of the pieces in the rings are tiny grains of dust, while others can be much larger. The rings are very wide, but they are surprisingly thin compared with their overall size.

Saturn has many moons, including Titan, its largest moon. Titan is especially interesting because it has a thick atmosphere and lakes and seas made of liquid methane and ethane. Saturn also has many other fascinating moons, including Enceladus, which has an ocean beneath its icy surface.
