Facts About Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is an ice giant. It has a blue-green color because methane gas in its atmosphere absorbs red light. Uranus is very cold and has a thick atmosphere made mostly of hydrogen, helium, and methane.

Uranus is famous for rotating on its side. Its axis is tilted about 98 degrees, which means that Uranus essentially rolls around the Sun as it travels through space. Uranus also has a system of faint rings surrounding the planet.

Uranus has many moons, and several of them are named after characters from the works of William Shakespeare and Alexander Pope. Uranus takes about 84 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun. Because of its unusual tilt, some parts of Uranus can experience extremely long seasons.
